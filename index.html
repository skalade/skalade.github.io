Should you abuse your coding agent?

If you’re anything like me you often find yourself at night yelling obscenities at your coding agent. The kind of stuff you wouldn’t say somewhere with an HR department. After one of my more heated moments I had a thought… Am I making it worse? If I did this to a human it would be considered abuse, and I’m fairly sure it wouldn’t improve their productivity. And yet the internet says the opposite. Sergey Brin said on a podcast that “not just our models but all models – tend to do better if you threaten them”.
